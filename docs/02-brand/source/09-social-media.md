## Export so it stays sharp

- **PNG** for anything with text, logos or flat colour. JPG only for photos.
- sRGB colour profile, never CMYK, for screens.
- Upload from a desktop browser. Phone apps compress harder.
- Don't zoom in Facebook's reposition tool; it upscales.

## What we measured

- A peer agency uploads post graphics as PNG at 1080 × 1350; Facebook delivers them as JPG at the same size.
- Their cover is stored as PNG at 960 × 407 and shown at 1250 × 530.
- So upload at full display size or larger, and expect Facebook to re-compress.
