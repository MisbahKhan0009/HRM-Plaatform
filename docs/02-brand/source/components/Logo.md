# Logo

The approved logo versions on their grounds, and the approved backgrounds: the primary lockup on Ink, the light version on white, the cube mark on Ink; white versions on photos and on red, a single-colour version for one-colour print.

- Use the files in the **Logos** asset group as they are: never recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects.
- The white and single-colour versions are filter previews of the light lockup, not approved artwork: ask the designer for real files before using them.
- Keep clear space equal to one cube's height on every side; respect `logo-min-lockup` and `logo-min-cube`, and switch to the cube mark below them.
