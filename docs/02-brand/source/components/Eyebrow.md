# Eyebrow

The eyebrow pill: mono caps with a red dot and a hairline border, fully rounded, that sits above a headline to name the brand, a series or a category.

- **Provide:** `children`, one to three words ("Big-Matrix Solutions", "Careers", "Case study", "Partner briefing"); the component sets them in capitals. `dot={false}` hides the dot.
- Spec: `eyebrow` style (500 12px/1 mono, tracking +16%) in `text`, on a `ground` fill; 9px × 16px padding; 1px `line-strong` border; `bm-r-pill` corners; a 7px `accent` dot 10px before the words.
- One eyebrow per headline, directly above it and left-aligned with it.
- In large graphics (posts, slides), scale the whole pill with the layout; keep its proportions.
