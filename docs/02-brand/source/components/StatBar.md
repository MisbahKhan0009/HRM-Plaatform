# StatBar

A row of numbers in equal cells, outlined in `line-strong`, each with a 20px red tick sitting on the bar's top edge.

- **Provide:** `stats`, an array of `{ value, label }`. Use real, approved numbers only; the brand's proof points are 6 systems in production, 5 clinical partners and 24/7 support coverage.
- Spec: up to 620px wide on the `ground`; `bm-r-lg` corners; cells 18px × 20px with 1px `line-strong` dividers; values in the `stat` style (Inter Tight 600 28px/1, −3%, tabular figures) in `text`; captions in the `caption` style (mono 10.5px caps, +12%) in `text-3`, 4px below.
- Two to four stats per bar. Never pad a row with soft numbers.
