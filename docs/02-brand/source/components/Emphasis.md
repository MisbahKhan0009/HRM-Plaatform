# Emphasis

The red emphasis word: the one word in a headline that carries the idea, filled with `bm-gradient-emphasis`.

- **Provide:** `children`, a single word inside a headline: "Built for <Emphasis>clinical</Emphasis> teams".
- At most one per headline, and only at headline sizes (`h3` and up): the gradient's weakest stop is 3.34:1 on either ground.
- Everything else in the headline stays `text` (Mist on Ink, Graphite on Paper).
- In the tagline, the emphasis word is *intelligent*.
- Where a browser cannot clip a background to text, the word falls back to solid Matrix Red.
